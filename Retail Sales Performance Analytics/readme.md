Retail Sales Performance Analytics
Project Overview

Retail organizations generate thousands of transactions every day, but operational data alone doesn't always provide actionable insights.
This project demonstrates how a Business Intelligence solution can transform raw retail sales data into interactive executive dashboards that support data-driven decision making.
The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales performance, identify profitable products, evaluate discount strategies, analyze regional performance, and compare business trends across different time periods.

Business Objective

The primary objective of this project was to design an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that enables decision-makers to:

- Monitor sales and profitability
- Track business performance over time
- Identify top and underperforming products
- Evaluate promotional effectiveness
- Analyze customer purchasing trends
- Compare historical business performance
- Support strategic decision-making through self-service analytics

Technologies Used

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Excel
- Interactive Visualizations

Business Value:
This dashboard consolidates multiple business KPIs into a single interactive reporting solution, reducing manual reporting effort and enabling faster identification of sales trends, profitability drivers, and product performance.
